FIELD REFERENCE

Method & safety

Suspected heatstroke is an emergency. Stop work, call 000 in Australia (or your local emergency number), and start cooling immediately. Follow your site’s emergency procedure.

Using a Kestrel

Let the meter stabilise at the work location and follow the model’s instructions. Enter air temperature for dry bulb and psychrometric wet bulb for wet bulb, not natural wet bulb or WBGT. Point the impeller into the airflow and use a representative reading, not a gust. Some Kestrel impellers can under-read at very low air speeds; verify with a suitable instrument if needed.

What the three-reading screen can tell you

It flags wet bulb above 32 °C or dry bulb above 44 °C using Brake & Bates (2002), Table II. A reading exactly at either cutoff needs a recheck. Wind does not change these temperature triggers. A reading below both cutoffs does not establish safe conditions.

What Full TWL needs

Use measured black globe temperature (150 mm or manufacturer-reported equivalent) and actual station pressure (not pressure corrected to sea level). Full TWL estimates a sustainable metabolic rate in W/m² of body surface area for healthy, hydrated, acclimatised people who can self-pace. This independent reconstruction uses the 2002 model and a light workwear profile (0.35 clo; vapour permeation 0.45). Do not apply it to impermeable or encapsulating PPE. Estimates outside 60–380 W/m² are not reported as exact values.

Below 115 W/m²
Withdrawal: no routine work
115 to below 140 W/m²
Buffer: escalate controls
140–220 W/m²
Acclimatisation: review controls
Above 220 W/m²
Higher band: no clearance implied

Why there is no countdown

TWL is not a duration. This app shows zero routine minutes for withdrawal only. All other times require a site-approved work/rest plan accounting for the task, PPE, recovery conditions and worker factors. The paper’s reference bands are not regulatory limits. Meter uncertainty can change the band near a boundary; recheck whenever conditions change. Have an occupational hygienist or site safety lead review the tool before operational use.
